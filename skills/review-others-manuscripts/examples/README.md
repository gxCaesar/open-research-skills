# Worked examples

The examples in this directory are synthetic. They were written to demonstrate report
structure and severity calibration, and no part of them comes from a real submission.

Real referee reports are confidential. A manuscript under review, the report written
about it, and the correspondence around it cannot be redistributed as teaching material,
and a report anonymised by removing names is still the authors' unpublished work.

That constraint is why this skill teaches the method rather than shipping a corpus of
prior reports, and why it carries no individual reviewer's personal style.
