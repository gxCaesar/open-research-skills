# Conference manuscript lifecycle

## Reconnaissance before prose

Resolve the venue, track, year, stage, submission system, and official authority. Open
the current author instructions, policy pages, template or author kit, checklist, and
stage-specific notice. Record the date and retain any material conflict. A prior-year
profile may suggest what to verify but cannot establish a current limit or permission.

Inventory the canonical source, bibliography, rendered PDF, supplement, figures, code
or data material, checklist, disclosure record, prior decision, and reviewer comments.
Label absent items and unobserved portal fields instead of inferring them.

## Draft from a locked contract

Before drafting, freeze the paper's question, one-sentence claim, contribution type,
method, data and split, comparators, metrics, independent unit, results, uncertainty,
limitations, and artifact anchors. Changes to any of those fields return upstream for
scientific review.

Give every section and paragraph one job. A useful paragraph contract is:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Question | The local issue the paragraph resolves |
| Evidence | Exact result, definition, source, or artifact |
| Interpretation | What follows and no more |
| Boundary | Limitation, condition, or transition when needed |

Draft only where current policy and author authorization permit it. Never fill a gap
with a plausible number, citation, experiment, or disclosure. Use explicit markers for
missing evidence.

## Polish without scientific drift

For one section, a section-by-section pass or continuation, use
[section polish and continuation](section-polish-workflow.md). It adds a resumable
queue and actual section acceptance without changing the scientific or venue boundary.

Create a reverse outline from the current text. Remove duplicated setup, unused
acronyms, promotional adjectives, module inventories, and generic claims of importance.
Prefer local edits over wholesale rewrites. Compare all edited quantitative sentences
against their source tables or logs and preserve qualifiers and citation scope.

## Camera-ready and package

Start from the accepted or author-approved baseline. Build a ledger of required venue
changes, promised response changes, author-approved optional changes, and prohibited
scientific changes. Reconcile source, PDF, supplement, checklist, disclosures, and
public artifact references. Obtain current final-stage rules; anonymous-submission
rules do not automatically carry over.

Keep external actions separate from local readiness. A locally valid package has not
been uploaded, saved, or submitted.
