# Title audit

The abstract is primary. Review the title only when requested, after fixing the dominant
scientific question.

## Checks

- The title and abstract promise the same problem and contribution.
- Information-bearing nouns and verbs replace `novel`, `powerful`, `effective`,
  `unified`, `rethinking`, and similar framing words.
- Every `first`, superlative, robustness, generality, or causal claim is supported by
  the paper's actual comparison or design.
- A named-method title explains what the method changes or enables.
- A question title is used only when the paper genuinely answers that question.
- Acronyms appear only when they help recognition; do not invent a backronym.

## Review output

For each issue, report the exact phrase, its function, the evidence location, the
mismatch, and the smallest revision objective. Do not rewrite the title unless asked.
