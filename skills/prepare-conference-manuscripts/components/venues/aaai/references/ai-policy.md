# AAAI-27 AI-policy conflict

As checked on 2026-09-04, two official AAAI-27 pages give materially different author
instructions:

- the Main Technical Track call says authors may judiciously use generative AI tools in
  preparing scholarly manuscripts while remaining responsible for the material;
- the dedicated author-policy page says papers containing LLM-generated text are
  prohibited unless that text is part of experimental analysis, while editing or
  polishing author-written text is allowed.

Status: `OFFICIAL_CONFLICT`.

Do not silently choose the broader interpretation. Until the venue clarifies the
conflict, this skill applies the narrower boundary:

- allowed: format and anonymity checks, source verification, evidence and claim maps,
  outlines and paragraph jobs, diagnosis, and editing or polishing author-written text;
- not allowed: generating new insertion-ready manuscript passages, abstracts,
  responses, or camera-ready prose for submission;
- always required: author responsibility for accuracy, originality, citations, and
  compliance; an AI system is not an author or citable source.

For a real paper, check both pages, the current author kit, institutional policy, and any
written clarification from the program chairs. Record the date and exact resolution.
If the conflict persists, keep the conservative boundary.
