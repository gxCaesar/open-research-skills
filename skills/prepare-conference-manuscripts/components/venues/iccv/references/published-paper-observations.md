A list of titles is not writing evidence. It shows the listing exists and gives a
starting point; it says nothing about how these papers organise a method section, place
their figures, or carry a claim through to their experiments. No structural observation
is recorded here, and none should be inferred from the titles.

When a first-party proceedings PDF can be opened, record it as empirical writing
evidence only. It never defines venue policy, mandatory section order, or a replacement
for the current author guidelines.
