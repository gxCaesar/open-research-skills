# ICLR 2027 AI-use record

The checked author policy requires authors to state how LLMs were used in both the paper
and submission form. The mandatory paper section does not count toward the page limit.
Authors remain responsible for every claim and artifact.

Create a factual activity record before drafting the statement:

| Activity | Tool and version if known | Inputs and outputs used | Human verification | Policy category |
|---|---|---|---|---|

The official page lists required-disclosure activities such as developing theories or
conceptual frameworks, mathematical claims or proofs, hypotheses, methodology and
experiment design, method implementation, translation, data cleaning, qualitative
analysis, and result interpretation. It recommends disclosure for additional activities
including figures, code editing, drafting or editing paper text, literature work,
brainstorming, references, structure, title, and keywords.

Reopen the live list rather than relying on this summary. State non-use only when the
authors have checked their actual workflow. For each material use, explain the task and
how authors verified the output. Do not list an AI system as an author, transfer author
responsibility to it, or claim plagiarism/factual review that was not performed.
