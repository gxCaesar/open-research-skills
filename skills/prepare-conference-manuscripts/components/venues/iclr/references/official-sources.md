The checked guide requires a genuine abstract, freezes additions to the author set after
the abstract deadline, and requires anonymous main and supplementary material. It sets
nine main-text pages for submission and ten for discussion revisions and camera ready.
References do not count; appendices follow the bibliography and may be unlimited, but
reviewers need not read them. The paper must contain an AI-use statement. Its official
example format need not be followed exactly, so a particular heading is not the only
valid disclosure form. Ethics and
reproducibility statements are recommended under the stated conditions and do not count
toward the main-text limit.
