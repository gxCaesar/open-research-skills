# AI-asset disclosure

Use a disclosure similar to:

> Illustrative icons were generated with an AI image model and subsequently curated, background-removed, colour-harmonized and assembled by the authors. Scientific content, labels, data-derived graphics, interpretation and final figure layout were authored and verified by the authors.

Adapt the wording to the target journal's current policy. Do not describe data-derived structural or quantitative graphics as AI generated when they were rendered from original analysis artifacts.
