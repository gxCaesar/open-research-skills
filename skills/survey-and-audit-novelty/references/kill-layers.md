# Adjudicating collisions

A collision report lists candidates. Turning one into a kill requires naming the layer it
lands on, and most reported kills land on a shallower layer than claimed.

## The three layers

**Framing.** The phrasing of the question is occupied. The data, the task and the method
asset survive. Rephrase and continue. This layer is misreported as a dead direction more
often than any other.

**Data.** The observations the hypothesis needs cannot be obtained. This requires the
enumerated source list and the joint-count table. One empty repository is not this layer.

**Task.** Measured headroom is at or below the measured noise floor, on this task and
this representation. This is the only layer that kills the asset, and it requires
measurement rather than argument.

## Scope discipline

A collision report gives verdicts at more than one scope. The headline verdict is written
at the widest scope in the report, and the widest scope is the one most likely to be
occupied. Read the per-candidate verdicts before the headline, and score every kill
against the specific claim it touches.

A collision against a narrow proposition demotes that proposition to a restatement with a
citation. It does not propagate outward to the direction.

## The repair table

Before reporting a set of kills, list them and ask for each what one available correction
would reverse it: a different search angle, a coarser matching level, a different lane, a
narrower claim.

A correction counts as a reversal only if it is established. Record each entry as
established, with what establishes it, or as asserted, and keep asserted entries out of
the fraction. "There must be some correspondence upstream" is a hypothesis about the
world; counting it as a reversal produces a calibrated-looking fraction resting on
nothing, and the table's shape makes that harder to see rather than easier. The failure
mode the table exists to catch — search fatigue reported as literature — is reproduced
exactly by an optimistic reading of what a correction would find.

| Reversible fraction | Reading |
|---|---|
| Nearly all | The audit measured search fatigue, not the literature. Redo it |
| About half | Mixed. Report the reversible ones as open questions, not as kills |
| Almost none | The kills are load-bearing. Report them |

Report the fraction alongside the kills. A kill list without it cannot be calibrated by
anyone else.

## Separating the verdicts

Record `scoop_verdict` and `venue_fit` as separate fields with separate reasons. Both may
recommend stopping and they fail for different repairs: a collision is repaired by
changing the contribution, a venue-fit doubt by changing the venue or the framing.
Reporting a venue-fit doubt as a collision removes that choice from the author.
