# Editor pack and cover letter

## Build the editor pack from an inventory

List every item required by the current journal and article type. Typical candidates
include manuscript source and PDF, title page, figures, source data, tables,
supplementary files, reporting checklist, cover letter, author declarations, ethics
information, competing interests, data and code statements, related manuscripts, prior
dissemination, and reviewer suggestions. The live requirements decide the actual set.

For each item record its filename, version, owner, readiness, required format, and
cross-references. Reconcile title, short title, authors, affiliations, corresponding
author, abstract, keywords, figure order, supplementary labels, declarations, and
availability language across all artifacts.

## Write a factual cover letter

A useful cover letter gives:

- the exact manuscript title and article type;
- one concise scientific question and supported contribution;
- why that contribution fits this journal and audience;
- any editor-required declarations about originality, related manuscripts, prior
  dissemination, authorship approval, ethics, competing interests, data, and code;
- requested handling information supported by policy and author facts.

Do not repeat the abstract, flatter the editor, overstate novelty, imply endorsement,
or claim all authors approved unless that was confirmed. Do not invent reviewer names,
expertise, conflicts, email addresses, exclusions, repository availability, or
exclusive-submission status.

## Presubmission versus submission

A presubmission inquiry, cover letter, and portal metadata are different artifacts.
Apply the requested format and stage. Keep external contact and portal writes separate
from drafting; local readiness does not authorize sending or submission.
