# Drafting and evidence-preserving polish

For Nature-family tasks, use [section-specific writing](nature-section-style.md) for
the sections being revised. It covers voice, title, abstract, Introduction, Results,
Methods, Discussion, legends and supplementary text without imposing a fixed template.

## Calibrate the publication form before the prose

Resolve the publication form separately from the scientific contribution. An Article,
Brief Communication, Comment, and News or Editorial item have different narrative jobs;
do not force all of them through an Article Results arc. Mark an incomplete or internally
inconsistent source `source-limited` rather than inferring its missing structure.

When the author requests Nature-family exemplar calibration, read
`nature-family-article-shapes.md`. Use its patterns only when the current evidence shape
matches. Keep observations from published papers, editorial heuristics, and current
official requirements visibly separate.

For Nature-family paragraph revision and legend drafting, use
[paragraph and legend lessons](nature-paragraphs-and-legends.md). It gives located
examples of mixed comparator outcomes, local validation limits, scientific transitions
and nested sampling. Use these when relevant even if the author did not name a paper.

## Draft from paragraph jobs

For full-paper ordering or disconnected sections, read
[whole-paper argument cases](whole-paper-argument-cases.md). Use the relevant case's
question-to-evidence dependencies, not its headings or number of figures.

Begin with a locked scientific contract and a section-level argument map. Assign each
paragraph one primary job:

| Job | Required content |
|---|---|
| Question | The concrete issue being resolved |
| Evidence | Exact method, result, display, or source |
| Interpretation | What the evidence supports |
| Boundary | Condition, limitation, or transition when needed |

Order definitions before use and evidence before broad interpretation. Methods should
make the producer, inputs, masks or exclusions, analysis unit, outputs, and evaluation
route reconstructable. Results should answer questions rather than narrate every run.
Discussion should distinguish result, interpretation, alternative explanation,
limitation, and implication.

Before drafting Results, make a figure-argument map: section question, display locator,
observed comparison, justified claim, next unresolved dependency, and placement reason.
Use it to make each block unlock the next scientific decision. Do not require one
paragraph per figure or a fixed number of figures.

Map every headline claim and decisive control across main, Extended Data,
Supplementary, table, and source-data locations. Material outside the main article may
carry robustness, calibration, additional cases, or diagnostics only when its absence
does not hide a qualifier that changes the main claim. Do not assume that every journal
or article form uses Extended Data.

Never fill a missing transition with invented science. Mark unresolved values,
citations, permissions, and author decisions explicitly.

## Polish with a reverse outline

For one section, a section-by-section pass or continuation, use
[section polish and continuation](section-polish-workflow.md). It tracks the actual
candidate, section acceptance and next item without replacing this writing guidance.

For each existing paragraph, write its current job in one line. Merge duplicate jobs,
split paragraphs with incompatible jobs, and remove setup that is not used later. Then
repair local sentences. Prefer ordinary causal prose over promotional adjectives,
module inventories, repeated openings, and stacked clauses.

Preserve numerical values, units, comparison direction, uncertainty, negation,
population or domain scope, citation scope, and scientific qualifiers. Check each
quantitative sentence against the exact table, figure, source-data file, or log. A
smooth rewrite that changes any of these is a scientific change, not polish.

Require the Discussion to point to at least one evidenced applicability boundary,
alternative explanation, or missing validation when such a boundary exists. Generic
future-work language is not a substitute for a limitation tied to the data, measurement,
comparison, or intervention.

## Abstract and title

Write them after the central claim is stable. The title should identify the object and
contribution without unsupported priority or causal language. The abstract should
compress the problem, gap, approach, evidence, main result, and boundary. Apply current
journal structure and length rules; bundled presets are editorial aids only.
