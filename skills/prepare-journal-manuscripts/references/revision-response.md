# Journal revision response

## Build one complete comment matrix

Create one row per editor and reviewer comment, retaining hierarchy and exact wording:

| ID | Source | Exact comment | Type | Existing evidence | Decision | Response | Manuscript change | Clean location | Marked location | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classify comments as factual correction, clarification, missing comparison,
methodological threat, requested experiment, claim or scope dispute, reporting or
policy issue, presentation issue, or editorial requirement. Address the
decision-critical items first while accounting for every item.

## Choose a defensible disposition

- accept and correct;
- clarify with existing evidence;
- add completed evidence under a frozen protocol;
- disagree with a specific technical reason and source;
- resolve partially and state the remaining gap;
- accept a limitation and narrow the claim.

Do not describe planned work as completed. Cost and authorize new work before promising
it. Report negative or inconclusive additions. Do not relabel post-hoc work as
prespecified, tune on a locked test, change the metric after viewing results, or hide an
unfavorable analysis.

## Synchronize three revision artifacts

The response letter, clean manuscript, and marked manuscript must agree. Verify every
claimed change at the cited location in both manuscript versions. Ensure every material
manuscript change is explained in the response. Reconcile updated figures, tables,
supplement, declarations, statistics, data availability, and code availability.

Preserve the submitted baseline. Generate the marked version from the same revised
source or compare against the exact baseline; do not maintain two independently edited
manuscripts. Follow current editor instructions on formatting, anonymity, tracked
changes, and permitted scope.

A journal revision or revise and resubmit package is not a conference rebuttal. The
editor letter defines whether new experiments, a restructuring, or a substantially
revised article are invited.
