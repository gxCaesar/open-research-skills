# Paper Card schema

Keep these sections once and in order. Translate headings when needed.

1. **Basic Information:** title, authors, venue, year, identifiers, code/data, source
   coverage, and reading date.
2. **One-Sentence Summary:** problem, approach, mechanism, and bounded result.
3. **Research Question:** concrete problem, importance, prior limitation, and precise
   question.
4. **Research Background and Development Path:** stages, approaches, tradeoffs, and the
   paper's claimed position; label external verification status.
5. **Core Pain Points:** manifestation, proposed cause, and evidence.
6. **Core Idea:** surface method, core insight, and `[Analysis]` transferable principle.
7. **Method Overview:** inputs, outputs, components, training or analysis, feedback, and
   assumptions.
8. **Core Module Breakdown:** function, rationale, input/output, supporting evidence, and
   measured or expected removal effect.
9. **Essential Formulas and Symbols:** formula, symbols, role, intuition, and locator;
   include only what is essential.
10. **Experimental Design and Evidence Chain:** data/population, split, scale, baselines,
    metrics, budget, uncertainty, each experiment, supported claim, and stronger
    unsupported claim.
11. **Correct Interpretation of Conclusions:** task and population scope, oracle inputs,
    end-to-end status, cost, dependence, hardest cases, uncertainty, and bounded result.
12. **Limitations Explicitly Acknowledged by Authors:** only explicit author statements,
    with source. If none are present, say so.
13. **Critical Analysis:** `[Analysis]` observation, alternative explanation, impact,
    falsifying test, and basis.
14. **Knowledge Learned:** transferable concepts, methods, formulas, or designs.
15. **Connections to Existing Knowledge:** externally verified, user-supplied, or clearly
    marked candidate links.
16. **Research Ideas:** source observation, hypothesis, delta, initial method,
    validation, decision rule, failure modes, budget, and innovation status.

Every substantive paper-derived statement needs a source pointer. Do not force a method-
paper table onto a theory, resource, clinical, or review paper; use `Not applicable` with
a reason.
