# Independent research-to-manuscript completion

Use this route for an authorized full research-to-paper request or manuscript mode.
It requires no other skill. The scientific stages still decide whether results support
a manuscript, a negative report or a return to intake. Do not create apparent completed
research by filling an outline when evidence is missing.

## Scope the actual deliverable

Read the current project authority, locked protocol, results and claim register. Preserve
canonical or frozen files; write a revision copy when new drafting is authorized. A
pilot-only, survey-only or status-only request does not request a manuscript. A full
request continues through source, figures, rendered pages and local packaging, not only
a handoff record. Separate manuscript readiness from the existing record validator's
handoff target: that target does not inspect the finished manuscript.

Open current official venue/year/track or journal/article-type instructions. Record the
actual template, page or word limits, anonymous versus identified stage, AI authoring
and image policy, required statements, source-data requirements and upload formats.
Do not substitute a generic template for the official final form. If a current form is
unavailable, produce an honestly labelled working draft and finish independent content
work; final-format readiness remains unverified.

## Draft from the actual evidence

1. Map every planned result paragraph and figure to the exact support already inspected.
   Keep comparator, population, split, metric direction, independent unit, uncertainty,
   exclusions and limitations attached to each claim. Reading a source register is not
   a substitute for reading the cited output.
2. Write the complete sections required by the selected article type. For an empirical
   method paper, a useful starting order is Introduction, Method, Experiments/Results,
   Discussion and Limitations; adapt the section order to the actual venue. Introduce
   the scientific problem, precise prior-work gap, method operation and evidence that
   tests that operation. A Nature-family Results section follows evidence questions,
   not a list of technologies. Do not impose three contributions or three aims.
3. Report reproducible Methods: data access/version, inclusion and exclusion, preprocessing,
   independent-unit split, baseline parity, fitting and selection, evaluation, software,
   resource details actually observed and uncertainty over the appropriate unit. Never
   change the frozen analysis to complete a statistical reporting field.
4. Use the [standalone figures](standalone-figures.md) route to create evidence plots and
   native schematics. Write full legends identifying panels, sample and replicate units,
   encodings, summary and interval definitions, tests when used and source-image treatment.
   Integrate exports at the actual template width and reconcile figure/text values.
5. Write title and abstract after the argument is stable. Include question, substantive
   method change, decisive observed result and bounded conclusion within current limits.
   Verify every citation by opening the primary source at the relevant claim location;
   keep unsupported prior-work statements out of the candidate.
6. Complete references, data/code availability, ethics/consent, competing interests,
   funding, author contributions and AI-use disclosure only from confirmed facts and
   current requirements. Never invent a repository URL, accession, grant or author agreement.

For a supported negative report, describe what failed under which protocol and its scope;
do not silently convert a failed method route into a benchmark or discovery claim.

## Render and complete the local candidate

Use the project's existing authoring toolchain and current official template. For a
LaTeX project, compile the actual entrypoint with its documented command; for Word or
another native document, use its local export route. An editable Markdown manuscript
is a working source, not evidence that a required PDF/DOCX exists. If the necessary
renderer is unavailable, keep the complete source and report the exact missing tool;
do not install software or claim rendering succeeded without authority and evidence.

Inspect all rendered pages at normal reading scale. Resolve concrete clipping, missing
glyphs, unreadable labels, stale figures, broken references and orphaned captions.
Compare main and supplement for matching populations and values. Audit strongest claims
against their source once, especially any apparent contradiction. A good layout cannot
repair unsupported evidence.

Deliver the complete editable manuscript, rendered candidate, figures and legends,
required supplement, references and required declarations. Add a factual cover letter
or response only when requested by the task/stage. For revision, address every reviewer
point and synchronize clean/marked copies and response locations; unrun experiments
remain unrun. Package from an explicit allowlist into a new local candidate directory,
excluding private records and workflow state. Link unresolved author-only fields in the
handoff outside the candidate rather than pretending it is submission-ready.

Optional conference, journal and visualization skills add detailed venue adapters,
specialist reporting validators or visual design. They do not supply missing evidence
or authority, and their absence does not stop this independent route. Public repository
creation, deposits, portal writes, sending responses and submission remain separate
explicitly authorized actions.
