## Authoring policy

The 2026 notice permits generative AI for tracking research developments or organizing
references only with human verification, responsibility, truthful declaration, and
required marking. It prohibits directly generated applications and unverified generated
content. Therefore this profile uses:

```text
mode: EVIDENCE_AND_AUDIT_ONLY
```

Permitted work includes source verification, evidence and claim records, candidate
comparison, argument mapping, section briefs, questions, and structured audit of
applicant-authored text. Do not generate insertion-ready application prose. The applicant
and institution remain responsible for interpretation, declarations, and submission.

## Format and application facts

- For the 2026 General Program and Young Scientists Fund (C), the reform page describes
  three body parts: rationale, research contents, and research foundation. It states that
  the body is in principle no more than 30 pages and that research contents are organized
  by the project's own scientific logic.
- The notice requires applicants to download and use the latest form from the information
  system. A locally recreated form does not verify current headings or fields.
- The applicant selects a research attribute based on the research content and must align
  application code, research direction, and keywords with current guidance and system
  choices.
- Applicant and participant information, authorship order, contribution, consent, ethics,
  security, and confidential material must be accurate and handled under the applicable
  current requirements.

These statements do not apply automatically to special programs, joint funds, another
year, or an institutional deadline. Treat those as separate current-source questions.
