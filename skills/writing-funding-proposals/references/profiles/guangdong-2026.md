# Guangdong Natural Science Fund 2026 profile

Checked 2026-09-04 against the official Guangdong Department of Science and Technology
notice. This is a dated routing snapshot, not a substitute for the attached project-type
guide, institutional instructions, live system fields, or a later call.

## Official source

[Notice on applications for the 2026 Guangdong Basic and Applied Basic Research Fund
Natural Science Fund](https://gdstc.gd.gov.cn/zwgk_n/tzgg/content/post_4718804.html),
published 2025-05-30.

The notice covers the 2026 General Program, Outstanding Young Scholar Program, and
Distinguished Young Scholar Program. It states applicant and host-institution conditions,
research-integrity and ethics requirements, limits on duplicate or overlapping
applications, cooperation requirements, and online application routing. The detailed
project-type requirements are in attachments referenced by the notice and must be opened
for a real application.

## Required live checks

Record a current direct source for each applicable item:

- exact project type, eligibility, affiliation, and application limits;
- funding amount, duration, budget method, cooperation, and attachment requirements;
- subject code, direction, keywords, and system fields;
- body structure, page or word limits, and the actual current form;
- institutional deadline and review procedure;
- ethics, security, confidentiality, applicant declarations, and authoring or AI policy.

This public snapshot did not establish a current generative-AI authoring permission from
the checked notice. Keep the policy `UNVERIFIED` and use
`EVIDENCE_AND_AUDIT_ONLY` until an applicable official and institutional source resolves
it. Do not carry a deadline, funding amount, page rule, code, or policy forward from a
prior proposal.
