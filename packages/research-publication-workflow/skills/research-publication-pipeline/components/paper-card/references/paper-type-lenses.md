# Paper-type lenses

Choose one primary lens and at most one substantial secondary lens.

## Methods, algorithm, or system

Reconstruct inputs, outputs, components, training requirements, external tools, feedback,
inference cost, and oracle inputs. Distinguish the core insight from the implementation
bundle. Check ablation isolation, baseline and backbone parity, leakage, compute budget,
model selection, and end-to-end status.

## Discovery or mechanism

Map observation, intervention, controls, replication, temporal order, and alternative
explanations. Separate association, necessity, sufficiency, and mechanism. Check model-
system limits, measurement validity, and independent replication.

## Resource, dataset, or benchmark

Inspect collection, inclusion/exclusion, annotation, preprocessing, splits, licence,
access, representativeness, missingness, label quality, contamination, leakage, update
policy, and benchmark incentives. Size alone does not establish usefulness or validity.

## Clinical or population

Record population, setting, enrollment, exposure or intervention, comparator, outcomes,
follow-up, missing data, and analysis set. Check prespecification, randomization,
blinding, confounding, multiplicity, calibration, external validation, attrition, adverse
events, and absolute as well as relative effects.

## Materials, chemistry, or engineering

Link composition, fabrication, operating conditions, characterization, structure,
property, mechanism, and performance. Check batch variation, uncertainty, durability,
scale-up, realistic conditions, metric normalization, and comparator parity.

## Review, perspective, or meta-analysis

Inspect scope, search and selection, inclusion/exclusion, taxonomy, synthesis, risk of
bias, heterogeneity, publication bias, and sensitivity analysis as applicable. For a
narrative review, distinguish evidence synthesis from author framing and advocacy.
