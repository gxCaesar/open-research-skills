# AAAI scientific review

Test whether the paper states one distinct AI contribution that matters beyond a narrow
recipe change. Review in this order:

1. question and contribution identity;
2. novelty against the closest technically relevant work;
3. soundness of assumptions, method, proof, data, split, and evaluation;
4. direct evidence for every abstract and conclusion claim;
5. reproducibility from materials supplied at submission time;
6. clarity and economy within the seven content pages.

Do not move load-bearing definitions, proofs needed for plausibility, protocol choices,
or decisive controls to a supplement merely to satisfy the page limit. A promise to
release code or data after acceptance is not present reproducibility evidence.

For author feedback, make a concern matrix with reviewer, exact concern, factual basis,
response objective, existing evidence, permitted manuscript change, and unresolved
limitation. Correct factual errors directly. Disagree with a reviewer only with a
specific artifact or argument; never invent a new result to make the response complete.
