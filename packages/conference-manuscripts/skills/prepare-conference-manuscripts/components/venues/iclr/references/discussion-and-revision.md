# ICLR discussion and revision

Create one row per reviewer concern:

| Concern ID | Exact concern | Type | Existing evidence | Response objective | Manuscript change | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classify the concern as factual error, missing explanation, missing comparison,
methodological threat, requested experiment, scope disagreement, or presentation issue.
Answer the decisive issue first. Cite exact revised sections, figures, tables, or
appendix items.

Before adding an experiment, freeze the hypothesis, protocol, comparison, independent
unit, metric, seed policy, and interpretation boundary. Report negative or inconclusive
results. Do not tune on a locked test, select seeds by outcome, change the metric after
seeing results, or present repeated runs as independent samples.

The checked 2027 guide permits paper revisions during public discussion and applies a
comparison to the original submission. Reviewers and area chairs may ignore changes
that are substantially different, and they need not read every upload. Preserve the
original PDF, communicate each material change, and prefer one coherent revision over
many incremental uploads.
