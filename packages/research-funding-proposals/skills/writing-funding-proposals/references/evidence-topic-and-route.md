# Evidence, topic selection, and route fit

## Source and claim records

Give each opened official or scientific source one row in `source-register.csv`. A
`VERIFIED` row names the issuer, year, program, source type, path or URL, access date,
clause locator, and exact scope. Use `search_query` only for a bounded `NOT_FOUND`
record.

Give every load-bearing claim a stable `CL*` row. Link it to one registered source and
record the source scope, verification state, applicant role, and last check. Separate:

- what has been completed and directly inspected;
- what capability can transfer and under which boundary;
- what is planned;
- what data, licence, access, result, or applicant evidence is missing.

A publication list supports bounded bibliographic facts only. Author position, venue,
publication count, and title do not establish contribution, relevance, influence, or
proposal readiness.

## Candidate portfolio before prose

For each credible candidate record:

- one precise scientific question and declared lane: `sota-method`, `discovery`, or
  `benchmark`;
- data feasibility, required variables, whether they coexist in linkable samples, and
  the independent statistical unit;
- access and licence state;
- nearest opened work and the testable delta;
- strongest relevant cheap baseline or alternative explanation;
- a comparison that can make the central claim less credible;
- failure criterion and action on failure;
- applicant fit and program fit supported by direct evidence;
- cheapest next check.

For empirical AI-for-biology projects, measure attainable headroom against the strongest
available representation and a fair cheap combined baseline before expensive method
development. Repeated model seeds are not additional biological or clinical units.

## Novelty and disposition

Interpret the scoop verdict inside the declared lane:

- `IDENTICAL`: the same discovery, the same method and margin under a matched protocol,
  or the same named benchmark gap is already established.
- `ADJACENT`: data, method family, or motivation overlaps but a material testable delta
  remains.
- `UNCERTAIN`: current opened evidence cannot distinguish the two; record the next
  bounded check.

Record the candidate disposition separately:

- `FRAMING`: the underlying task remains viable but the current claim is poorly framed;
- `DATA`: required data, linkage, licence, or independent units are not currently
  established;
- `TASK`: paired evidence closes the task-level headroom or validity.

Do not turn strong adjacent work into an identical-work verdict, or a data search miss
into proof that the task is impossible.

## Route and application-code comparison

Use current official labels. For each plausible route compare:

- the core new knowledge;
- the main output and validation endpoint;
- the natural reviewer community;
- title, direction, keywords, and code coherence;
- the strongest mismatch with the project;
- evidence state and unresolved system-field check.

For computational biology, AI for science, or digital health, an AI model, public
dataset, or GPU does not itself establish an information-science contribution. Compare
plausible computing, life-science, medical, and interdisciplinary routes. Do not produce
insertion-ready code-selection language while the fit remains `UNVERIFIED` or in tension.

Freeze the route only after the applicant confirms the selected contribution, scope, and
current system options.
