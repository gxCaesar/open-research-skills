# Worked whole-paper argument cases

Read when arranging a complete manuscript or repairing an outline whose sections are
individually readable but do not build one answer. These are located editorial cases,
not required article shapes. Contribution type and publication form are separate.

## Method: capability across constraints

Source: [diaTracer](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55448-8), Article,
publisher PDF pp. 1–12, main text, Methods and Figs. 1–7. Separate supplementary
files were not read for this case. Page numbers below are file pages.

**Observed:** the abstract promises an input transformation and supported analyses,
not a uniform performance win. The Introduction explains competing spectrum- and
peptide-centric strategies before identifying the ion-mobility and search-space problem.

| Main evidence | Question answered | Why the next block is different |
|---|---|---|
| Fig. 1, p. 3; workflow, p. 2 | How does the measurement become searchable spectra and then quantities? | An interface still needs a task-matched evaluation. |
| Fig. 2, p. 4; deep profiling, pp. 2–3 | Does routine protein profiling remain competitive? | Similar depth does not establish the value of flexible searches. |
| Fig. 3, p. 5; CSF, pp. 3–4 | Can altered cleavage rules and modification searches expose additional peptide information? | Another sample type tests whether the benefit survives a different regime. |
| Fig. 4, p. 6; plasma, pp. 4–5 | How do completeness and search choices affect downstream quantities? | A partly constrained search is not yet the nonspecific-search case. |
| Fig. 5, p. 7; HLA, p. 6 | Can the workflow handle a large nonspecific search space? | Sequence identification does not establish modification-site resolution. |
| Fig. 6, p. 8; phosphoproteomics, p. 7 | Can modified peptides and positional isomers be resolved and inspected? | These demonstrations do not settle low-input dependence on auxiliary data. |
| Fig. 7, p. 9; low input, pp. 8–10 | Which observations remain available without a high-input library? | Discussion can now explain applicability and method-specific limits. |

**Editorial inference:** datasets earn their place by testing distinct constraints.
Explain why the next dataset matters before introducing its name. A software-module
catalogue would sever the link between the abstract's capability promise and evidence.

Discussion (p. 10) explains the computational distinction, MS1 dependence, information
not yet used in scoring, runtime reuse and future acquisition support. Methods
(pp. 10–12) separates the common algorithm from dataset-specific recipes. This is an
organizational precedent, not authority for copying parameter values.

**Limit:** a competitor has greater protein depth in the direct-data comparison; the
hybrid condition uses additional data. Do not compress the narrative to "outperforms
existing tools across datasets." Unread supplementary diagnostics are not certified.

## Discovery and translation: increasing evidential strength

Source: [spatial proteomics and TEN](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08061-0),
Article, publisher PDF pp. 1–8 and 10–13, main text, Methods and Figs. 1–6.
Extended Data captions were consulted for placement; separate supplementary tables
were not audited.

**Observed:** the abstract moves from a disease question through cell-type-resolved
measurement, pathway evidence, intervention and patient observation. The article does
not use a standalone Results heading.

| Main evidence | Question answered | Next dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Fig. 1, p. 2; workflow and keratinocytes, pp. 2–3 | Which disease-associated signals occur in the affected compartment? | Immune cells could contribute different signals. |
| Fig. 2, p. 4; immune cells, p. 3 | What distinguishes immune responses across disease groups? | Pooled immune cells do not identify the subtype. |
| Fig. 3, p. 5; subtypes and spatial regions, p. 3 | Where and in which cell types is the response concentrated? | Localization alone does not establish an actionable pathway. |
| Fig. 4, p. 6; pathway evidence, pp. 3–5 | Do protein, transcript and phosphorylation observations converge? | Convergence still needs intervention to support functional importance. |
| Fig. 5, p. 7; models, pp. 5–6 | Does inhibition change the phenotype across tested models and schedules? | Preclinical effects do not determine patient outcomes. |
| Fig. 6, p. 8; patients, pp. 6–7 | What happened during the reported clinical courses? | Interpretation must retain their observational design. |

**Editorial inference:** transitions expose what the preceding experiment could not
decide. This is a scientific dependency, not simply "we next investigated." A small
patient-course figure can carry the last translation step after a much denser
experimental figure; panel count is not argumentative weight.

Methods separates sampling and treatment, model-specific experiments, acquisition,
analysis and orthogonal assays. Discussion (pp. 7–8) reconnects the pathway to disease
biology and calls for larger clinical trials. Extended Data carries additional assay,
model and patient evidence, not permission to remove main-text qualifications.

**Limit:** do not copy a causal verb backward onto localization or association. Do not
turn the patient series into randomized efficacy evidence, or import this clinical
ending into a nonclinical method manuscript.

## Resource: construction, usefulness and a bounded demonstration

Source: [PERISCOPE morphology atlas](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02537-7),
publisher-labelled Resource. Main text and legends: PDF pp. 1–13; Methods: pp. 14–19.
Extended Data captions were read; separate supplementary files were not.

**Observed:** the abstract links atlas construction, an enabling platform, functional
uses and one biological demonstration.

| Figure / PDF page | Argumentative job |
|---|---|
| 1 / 3 | Establish measurement-to-perturbation assignment. |
| 2 / 4 | Establish construction and agreement with known biology. |
| 3 / 6 | Examine environment-dependent profiles. |
| 4 / 7 | Test another cell line while retaining weaker signal. |
| 5 / 9 | Show feature-level interrogation as candidate generation. |
| 6 / 10 | Follow one candidate with orthogonal experiments. |

Methods defines profile construction and aggregation. Discussion retains coverage,
assignment and cell-context limits. Extended Data supplies technical support. The
paper acknowledges independent reports of the exemplar's biological role.

**Editorial inference:** separate resource usefulness from universal prediction
superiority and discovery priority. A validated example demonstrates a route to
investigation; it does not validate every atlas-derived hypothesis.

## Use the cases on a new manuscript

Reverse-outline what the draft actually establishes. For each Results block, write
its answer and the unresolved question that makes the next block necessary. Link the
figure, control and Methods definition. A transition requiring an unrun experiment is
a missing evidence link, not a prose problem.

Test placement with two editorial questions:

- If this block vanished, would the headline change or become unsupported? Its decisive
  evidence or qualification then belongs in the main argument.
- If two blocks changed order, what would the reader not yet know? If nothing changes,
  consider grouping them by the different limits they test instead of inventing a dependency.

Keep the strongest supported contribution positive and specific. For a resource with
mixed comparisons, lead with what users can inspect or do; report the comparisons
where they bound that use. Neither rename it a SOTA method nor make the abstract a
list of things the resource does not prove.

Reread the abstract without the paper: every promise needs an answer in the main text.
Then reread Discussion without the abstract: it should explain what the evidence
changes, not repeat the inventory. Apply `nature-section-style.md` for sentence-level
editing after this argument is coherent. Do not copy these cases' headings or counts.
