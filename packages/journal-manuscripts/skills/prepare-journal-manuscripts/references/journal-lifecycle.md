# Journal lifecycle and authority

## Reconnaissance

Resolve the journal, publisher, article type, collection or special issue, stage, and
submission system. Open the current journal instructions, article-type page, formatting
or template source, reporting-policy pages, data and code policies, image policy,
ethics and disclosure requirements, checklist, and stage-specific editor letter.

Record the source, date checked, applicable scope, and any conflict. Classify each rule
as journal-specific, publisher-wide, reporting-guideline, funder or institutional, or
repository-specific. Apply the narrowest applicable current authority. Live form fields
remain `UNVERIFIED` until an authorized user observes them.

## Lifecycle stages

Keep these states separate:

1. planning or presubmission inquiry;
2. initial submission;
3. technical check;
4. editorial assessment;
5. peer review;
6. minor or major revision;
7. revise and resubmit after rejection when explicitly invited;
8. acceptance and production;
9. correction or post-publication update.

Instructions and permitted changes differ by stage. Do not reuse initial-submission
anonymity, format, file, or declaration rules at acceptance without checking.

## Canonical artifact inventory

Identify the authoritative manuscript source and rendered candidate, title page,
figures and source data, tables, supplement, methods or reporting checklist, cover
letter, response letter, clean manuscript, marked manuscript, data and code statements,
ethics documents, author declarations, and production files. Record which were observed
and which remain missing or unverified.

Before final delivery, confirm that filenames, versions, titles, authors, affiliations,
figure numbering, supplementary references, declarations, and availability language
agree. A locally complete package has not been submitted or accepted.
