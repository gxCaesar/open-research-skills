## Policy versus implementation

Venue policy establishes what must be available and disclosed. FAIR and DataCite guide
how a record can be discoverable, citable, and reusable; they do not automatically make
every field a journal hard requirement.

For Nature Portfolio original research, the current common policy requires a Data
Availability statement and transparent access conditions for the minimum supporting
dataset. Large datasets are better placed in repositories than supplementary files, and
certain data types have mandatory community deposition. Reviewers may request supporting
data during evaluation. Restrictions can be legitimate but must be disclosed and
defensible.

Open the exact journal page, reporting summary, data-type mandate, repository terms, and
live submission form when they affect the statement. Record sources as `VERIFIED`,
`UNVERIFIED`, `COULD_NOT_OPEN`, or `NOT_FOUND`.
